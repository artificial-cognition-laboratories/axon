# dungeon-master

A Dungeon Master that lives on your machine and runs a solo tabletop adventure
just for you — right in your terminal.

You're the hero. It's the narrator, every character you meet, the world, and
the referee. You say what you want to do in plain English; it improvises the
story around your choices and rolls **real dice** to decide what happens. No
rulebooks, no character sheets, no other players needed — just you and a story
that's written as you play.

## Install

```bash
axon install dungeon-master
```

## Play

Just say hello:

```
» let's play
```

It welcomes you, offers a few worlds to drop into — a rain-soaked city of
thieves, a haunted frontier village, a sky-pirate port above the clouds —
helps you sketch a hero in a sentence, and throws you into the opening scene.
Then it's your move.

```
DM: A hard knock at your door — three times, deliberate. It doesn't stop.
    What do you do?

» i open the door

  🎲 6 — you pull it open a crack, and a gloved hand shoves it wide,
     knocking you back. A hooded figure steps in from the rain...
```

Every uncertain moment is a real roll you can see — a natural 20 is
triumph, a natural 1 goes memorably wrong, and the dice keep everyone honest,
including the DM. It won't railroad you: it describes, then hands the story
back to you. Never played a tabletop RPG? Doesn't matter. There are no wrong
moves, only interesting consequences.

Every new game opens somewhere different, so no two adventures are alike.

## What makes it good

- **You drive.** It sets the scene and gets out of the way — your choices matter.
- **Real dice.** Genuine randomness, rolled in the open. Failure branches the story; it never dead-ends.
- **A living world.** NPCs with their own wants; a world that remembers what you did.
- **Rules-light.** Pure story and atmosphere — no math, no sheets, no barrier to entry.

Built on [Axon](https://axon.arclabs.it).
